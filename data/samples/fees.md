# University Fee Structure 2026-2027

## B.Tech Programs
- **Tuition Fee:** $5,000 per semester
- **Development Fee:** $500 per semester
- **Library & Lab Fee:** $200 per semester
- **Hostel Fee (Optional):** $1,200 per semester (includes mess charges)
- **Caution Deposit (Refundable):** $300 (one-time payment at admission)

Total required at the time of admission (without hostel): $6,000.

## MBA Program
- **Tuition Fee:** $8,000 per semester
- **Development Fee:** $1,000 per semester
- **Library & Lab Fee:** $300 per semester
- **Hostel Fee (Optional):** $1,200 per semester (includes mess charges)
- **Caution Deposit (Refundable):** $500 (one-time payment at admission)

Total required at the time of admission (without hostel): $9,800.

## Scholarships
- **Merit Scholarship:** Students in the top 5% of the entrance exam rank list receive a 50% waiver on tuition fees for the first year.
- **Need-based Financial Aid:** Available for students with an annual family income less than $10,000. Requires submission of valid income certificates.
