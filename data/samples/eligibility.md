# Eligibility Criteria for University Admissions 2026

## B.Tech Computer Science
To be eligible for the B.Tech Computer Science program, applicants must meet the following criteria:
- Minimum of 75% aggregate in 10+2 (High School) Board Examinations.
- Must have studied Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as core subjects.
- Must have a valid rank in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Mains 2026.
- The application deadline is May 15th, 2026.

## MBA (Master of Business Administration)
To be eligible for the MBA program, applicants must meet the following criteria:
- A completed Bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 60% aggregate.
- A valid CAT/MAT/XAT score from the 2025/2026 session.
- Minimum 2 years of full-time work experience (preferred but not mandatory).
- The application deadline is June 30th, 2026.

## Reservation Policy
As per institutional guidelines, 15% of seats are reserved for SC candidates, 7.5% for ST candidates, and 27% for OBC candidates. A 5% relaxation in minimum aggregate marks is provided to candidates under these categories.
